Flagship Venture & Deep-Tech Startup

Aidra — Autonomous UAV Edge Intelligence

Eliminating the post-flight data bottleneck for commercial drone fleets through Spatiotemporal Knowledge Distillation and ultra-low-power onboard filtering.

Co-Founder & CTO • Bonn, Germany
🏛️ University of Bonn • CV Lab 🚀 Transfer Center enaCom

Commercial drone inspections generate 60+ GB of raw video per flight, forcing engineering teams into hours of manual screening or requiring heavy, battery-draining onboard GPUs. As Co-Founder & CTO, I architected Aidra's Two-Stage Onboard Cascade powered by Spatiotemporal Knowledge Distillation (SKD) — compressing dual spatial and temporal Teacher networks into a tiny INT8-quantized Student model that runs directly on a sub-0.5W microcontroller.

Stage 01 • Trigger

64×64px SKD Saliency

Continuous spatiotemporal anomaly screening at 50 FPS on a <0.5W MCU, discarding empty sky & healthy infrastructure.

Stage 02 • Verifier

224×224px ROI Verifier

High-precision defect verification on candidate regions for wind turbines, solar PV parks, bridges, and power grids.

Stage 03 • Telemetry

MAVLink & GIS Export

Synchronizes extracted defect crops with PX4/MAVLink GPS coordinates and exports GDPR/SORA-compliant reports.

Video Understanding • CVPR '22

PyABD — Action Boundary Detection

🏛️ University of Bonn • Video Understanding (2026)

Fast, completely unsupervised temporal action segmentation framework in PyTorch that detects action change points without frame-wise annotations.

  • Detects action boundaries via local minima in cosine feature similarity and refines segments with Weighted Merge Hierarchical Clustering.
  • Supports both Offline full-video evaluation (MoF/F1) and causal low-latency Online streaming mode.
PyTorch Unsupervised Learning Signal Processing SciPy
